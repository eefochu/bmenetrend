# Trip planner

The Map, Plan / Tervezés and Favorites destinations retain their screen state. The planner owns a separate ViewModel and StateFlow. Search combines indexed stops with cached Photon places; planning starts explicitly. Map polling pauses while the planner is visible. Changing endpoints, departure time or canceling a search cancels the old request, and old generations cannot overwrite new results.

## Operators and alternatives

The planner combines two sources:

- EMMA/MÁVPlusz routes include Volán (`BUS` / `COACH`), BKK, rail, metro, HÉV, tram and trolleybus. The general `TRANSIT` request has no operator blacklist. A separate `WALK + BUS + COACH` request finds useful bus alternatives even when EMMA ranks several train journeys first.
- `LocalTimetableRouter` searches the reviewed ViaBusz, KanyarGO and HOMM scheduled trips already shipped with the app. Local buses may form an entire journey or connect to EMMA routes at either end, including local → Volán → local trips. Local GPS positions without trip identities are never used to invent a route or a realtime timetable.

Online planning tries BKK first, including Volán/coach journeys it serves, with EMMA as fallback. BKK's router does not mind long walks and the Worker cannot ask it to avoid them, so when every BKK journey walks ten minutes or more, EMMA is also asked with a strong walking reluctance (`walkReluctance:5.0`), bounded to four seconds, and its journeys are merged in. This found e.g. Biatorbágy Innovatív Technikum → Iharos utca via 762 → 767 to Meggyfa utca, which both default queries hide. Independently, for up to two of the earliest BKK journeys that end with a walk of eight minutes or more, BKK is asked for a connection from the stop where they get off (from that arrival plus the usual three-minute transfer time); a connection sparing at least four minutes of walking is spliced in, so the rider waits for a connecting vehicle instead of walking the rest. Both searches run together within the same four-second bound, and the publication window waits up to that bound when such an improvement is possible. Searches consider all enabled operators, then publish **a practical recommendation and up to two useful, distinct backups together** (one or two cards when fewer are useful). The displayed plan remains unchanged until an explicit replan or a change to the inputs. Timetable and route source identities are preserved.

Practical ranking uses door-to-door time, walking weighted at three times waiting for the first ten minutes and five times beyond that (a rider would rather wait at the closest stop than walk, and a 20-minute walk is far worse than twice a 10-minute one), five minutes of preference cost per change, and three minutes of preparation preference per railway boarding (one minute for coach boarding). These costs affect ordering only: displayed walking, boarding and arrival times remain the source times. Public services take precedence over registration-required services. A much faster train can still beat the metro; no station pair or operator is hardcoded.

The recommendation must arrive within 25% of the earliest arrival's elapsed time, clamped to five–ten minutes. A journey that walks less may arrive later by up to two minutes per walking minute saved (at most forty-five minutes). Backups can arrive within one third of that elapsed time, clamped to fifteen–twenty minutes. Duplicate departures on the same line sequence and nearby boarding points collapse into one choice. Journeys riding the same vehicles (same lines, boarding within two minutes at nearby stops) that only differ in where they get off are one choice; the better one is kept. When some journey walks a sensible amount, journeys walking more than max(15 min, least walking + 10 min) are never offered. Backups may arrive later by up to two minutes per walking minute they save, never beyond forty-five minutes after the earliest arrival; this applies to the recommendation too, so a bus 25 minutes later beats a 38-minute walk. Online local/gateway search prunes paths with the same 45-minute bound (offline planning keeps twenty minutes). When some backup walks at least two minutes less than the recommendation, the least-walking one is always shown ("wait for a ride instead of walking"). Then, for variety, the best viable journey of each kind of service not yet on a card (Volán, local ViaBusz/KanyarGO/HOMM, BKK, rail) gets a card, cheapest kind first, as long as slots remain. Remaining slots go by practical ranking; the first backup is simply the next-best route, while a third card is skipped if it arrives five or more minutes after a shown one with no less walking, no fewer changes and no access gain. No card is ever shown that arrives no earlier than a shown one while needing an extra change or at least a minute more walking (e.g. the same bus reached the hard way). Slots are never padded.

Candidates remain private. Short local rides come from the local timetable search, which can finish after both direct answers, so once that search has started (local network loaded and local stops near an endpoint) a search does not publish until it reports, bounded to 2.5 seconds. A hanging network load never delays a good direct answer. After the local search, combined journeys (local ViaBusz/KanyarGO/HOMM buses joined to Volán, rail or BKK at a gateway stop, e.g. ViaBusz → Volán → ViaBusz) get their own bounded window of three seconds; the search finishing releases it at once. Gateway stops are preferred when a national stop is right next to them, and a local stop only counts as a rail interchange within 150 m of a platform (Biatorbágy Vasútállomás, not a school 330 m from the line). The few BKK walking requests go to the walks from the origin and to the destination first, nearest first (local stop-to-stop transfer walks come after), since a slower fallback estimate at a nearby stop can miss its bus by seconds. Walks of 40 m or less between an endpoint and a local stop (e.g. the destination is the stop itself) are filled in directly when walking services return nothing, so the rider is never taken one stop past it to walk back. Their middle segments use plain BKK-first routing without the slower walk-sparing searches, to keep the request count bounded. The planner screen starts loading the local timetables as soon as it opens and keeps them for the day, so the first search after app start can already offer short local rides. The two direct queries can complete together; the first usable answer starts one bounded 500 ms collection window for another useful choice. Local and gateway work does not prolong this timer. Once selected, both cards are published atomically and do not change until an explicit replan or input change. Outstanding local/gateway HTTP is cancelled. The overall online deadline is ten seconds; this bounded search does not claim a globally optimal journey.

Recommendations exclude gaps longer than sixty minutes, including initial waits before walking and waiting for a local bus or transfer. Walking and riding durations are not counted as waits. Choosing a later departure plans from that actual time. Generic boarding/transfer feasibility and BKK router provenance checks remain intact.

The compact diagram displays movement only: walking and riding. Consecutive, contiguous walking legs are combined for readability; waiting gaps do not become bars or add walking minutes. Total elapsed time still includes waiting, shown separately as “várakozás” in the detailed steps. Walking never counts as a vehicle boarding or transfer.

## Local timetable validity

`TransitNetwork.verifiedProviderIds` records successful HTML/PDF fingerprint validation independently of today's service date. It is cached in the optional JSON field of the existing schema and unioned when networks merge. Older caches without proof are revalidated before planning. A changed source clears verification; an ordinary non-operating day does not. This matters when planning on Saturday for a later working day.

The local router selects each trip's school-term/break calendar for the requested service date, including public holidays and working-day transfers. It also examines previous-day overnight trips and the next service day within a 24-hour search horizon. Times above 24:00 retain their original service date. Wall-clock times are converted in Europe/Budapest; nonexistent or repeated DST times are skipped rather than guessed.

The imported calendars are reviewed through December 31, 2026. Beyond that range local rides are unavailable and coverage is marked partial. The national planner still uses EMMA's published timetable horizon. On Saturday, October 24, 2026, ViaBusz and KanyarGO have no regular service in these imports; HOMM uses its weekend/school-break timetable. Restricted school services retain their registration-required notice.

## Connections and walking

All access, egress and transfer walking durations come from EMMA's street router. Geographic distance only chooses candidate links and gateways; a missing street route never falls back to a straight-line walking estimate. Walks are directed, routed in aliased batches of at most eight, and cached by both endpoint coordinates. At most three EMMA HTTP requests run concurrently across walking and national searches.

Local access and egress candidates are within 1,200 metres of the original endpoint. Local platform-transfer candidates are within 200 metres. Exact reviewed shared platforms use the existing canonical IDs; proximity never merges unrelated stops, platforms or departure boards. A local search maintains separate earliest-arrival labels by stop and boarding count and scans every applicable local trip. Actual boarding/alighting stops, intermediate stops, source times and calendar restrictions are preserved.

For combined journeys, the planner evaluates up to three distinct gateways at each end, near national stops or boarding/alighting points returned by the source. Candidate selection considers both rail and road interchanges. It searches national continuations between the original endpoints and these local gateways, including combinations with a local leg at both ends. If the map stop index is still loading, a bounded EMMA area query supplies interchange candidates independently. This bounded mobile search does not claim to enumerate every possible national/local interchange or guarantee the globally optimal journey.

## Timing and presentation

Leave now adds two minutes of preparation. Choosing a date and time uses that exact start time. Walking speed is 1.15 m/s. Initial boarding requires 60 seconds and changing vehicles requires 180 seconds in addition to transfer walking. The national request also applies 30 seconds of alighting slack and omits canceled services. Local/national boundaries receive the same transfer allowance; final assembled journeys are checked again. Staying aboard a through service does not create a transfer. At most five boardings / four transfers are allowed overall.

Source times are preserved; overlapping legs, missed departures and impossible transfers are rejected. Only source-marked realtime legs receive a live label. Local timetables are scheduled. Cards and detail timelines show walking, waiting, line badges, source times and intermediate stops without inventing intermediate arrival times. Past journeys prompt replanning.

Origin/destination coordinates and departure time are sent to EMMA. Local walking links and gateway coordinates are also sent to route those connections. Using current location includes those GPS coordinates. Photon receives search text, not GPS location. No additional account, private API key or backend is needed.

## Validation

Deterministic tests cover national train/metro/bus responses, the formerly hidden Volán alternative, all three local operators, local → Volán → local combinations, missed connections, source failures, partial walking aliases, directed walking caches, source verification and cache round trips, Saturday calendars, future-date planning, overnight trips, DST conversion, restricted school services and cancellation.

A live end-to-end check on October 24, 2026 verified the reported Kamaraerdő–Biatorbágyi Innovatív Technikum journey with Volán 760 / 762 and HOMM 288 connections. All three local source document fingerprints passed validation. Only a trimmed public-stop-to-school Volán response is committed in `app/src/test/resources/planner/volan-weekend.json`; the user's private access address and live harness are excluded. The live check is separate from the deterministic test suite.

EMMA uses the [OpenTripPlanner plan protocol](https://docs.opentripplanner.org/api/dev-2.x/graphql-gtfs/queries/plan). Its deployed query fields and bus/coach and walking mode combinations were checked against the live endpoint.
